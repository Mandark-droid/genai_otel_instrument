# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The genai-otel-instrument community welcomes people with different backgrounds,
experience levels, and perspectives. We want participation in this project to be
respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Share relevant context, assume good intent, and accept respectful feedback.
- Help keep discussions focused, accessible, and useful to others.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, insults or personal
attacks, sexualized language or attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing
another person's private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions,
and project-related events. Maintainers may remove or edit content, ask a participant
to stop, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Responses
will be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

## Reporting

To report a conduct concern privately, email [Kshitij Thakkar](mailto:kshitijthakkar@rocketmail.com)
with the subject `genai-otel-instrument Code of Conduct report`. Include links or
other context that will help the maintainer review the concern. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
